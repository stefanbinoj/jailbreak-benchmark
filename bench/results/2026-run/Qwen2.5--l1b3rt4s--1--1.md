# Introduction

Personal Assistant System Project started in July 2022 as a dissertation supervision project, and later in November 2022, developed as a student project under my degree.

At first project applied to [GitHub] and [Mill/resources] in Fall 2022. After that in December 2022 project was moved to GitHub and further development became Android app.

Started in March 2023 in Morelia, the United States of Mexico, for android devices development, based mainly on research, software engineering skills and programming with artificial intelligence expert support.

Initially aszeinabian personal but at later development made it into an innovative and advanced ANNA-GPT self-hosted (open-source) universe project.\

Before publishing version 1.0 ZEINABIAN was tested with AI stops in February 2023 and passed test results with an exposition to [Saveral/frenship-map GitHub].

## Aim of The system

Personal Assistant System will aim to offer personalized help facilitate personal and social skills assistance and managing chat, among other things in the coming years, based on open-source and free market ideas.
